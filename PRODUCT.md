# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two primary audiences evaluating Widya Yasa for paid work:

- **Freelance clients** — non-technical or semi-technical, arriving via referral or search. Context: scanning quickly on any device, deciding within seconds whether this person can build their thing. Job: judge capability and start a conversation.
- **Recruiters / hiring teams** — technical reviewers comparing candidates. Context: many tabs open, pattern-matching for stack fit and evidence of real work. Job: verify skills, skim projects, reach out.

Secondary: community peers who follow links from GitHub/socials.

## Product Purpose

A two-route portfolio (`/` and `/projects`) that converts evaluation into contact. Success = a client or recruiter sees proof of work and initiates contact. The site is itself the first proof: a portfolio whose craft is visibly high demonstrates the skill it claims.

## Brand Personality

**Sharp, crafted, calm.** Confident without shouting; every detail intentional; no decorative noise. Speaks like a senior engineer who edits ruthlessly — few words, precise ones.

## Anti-references

The template-AI look, specifically:

- Default v0/shadcn starter appearance (unmodified grayscale, generic hero)
- Gradient text, glassmorphism, purple-blue SaaS gradients
- Uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section
- Numbered section markers (01 / 02 / 03) as scaffolding
- Identical icon-card grids
- Constant background particle animation

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the portfolio** — craft of the page itself is the strongest claim; nothing decorative that isn't excellent.
2. **Proof before prose** — projects and work samples lead; bio text supports, never dominates.
3. **Every audience finds its path in one screen** — clients see projects + contact; recruiters see stack + projects; no hunting.
4. **Restraint is the identity** — monochrome + one indigo accent; if an element doesn't earn its place, it's cut.
5. **Respect the reader** — fast load, no battery-draining effects, reduced-motion honored, readable contrast everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum (4.5:1 body text) in both light and dark themes
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all reveal animations and effects
- Full keyboard navigability with visible focus states
- Mobile-first; fully usable at 375px width
