# Context: my-website

Glossary for Widya Yasa's personal portfolio site.

## Terms

### Portfolio

The site as a whole. A personal website whose primary audiences are **freelance clients** and **recruiters** (full-time hiring). Not a blog platform, not a community hub.

### Featured Project

A curated project (3–4 max) shown on the home page. Hand-picked to impress freelance clients and recruiters. Always a subset of the Project Index. Avoid: "highlight", "showcase item".

### Project Index

The `/projects` page — the complete list of projects, not just featured ones. Every Featured Project also appears here.

### Contact CTA

The call-to-action driving visitors to initiate contact. Lives as a section on the home page and in the footer. There is no dedicated `/contact` page.

### Site map

Two routes only: `/` (all sections) and `/projects` (Project Index). `/blog` and `/contact` are removed.
